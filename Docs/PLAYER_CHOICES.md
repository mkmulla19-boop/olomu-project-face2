# PLAYER CHOICES

**Status:** Framework only.

---

## Choice Categories

The system should eventually support:

- Dialogue choices
- Moral choices
- Faction choices
- Resource choices
- Rescue / abandon decisions
- Trust decisions
- Exploration decisions
- Hidden choices
- Long-term consequences

---

## Design Principles

- Choices should not always produce immediate rewards.
- Some consequences may appear much later.
- Choices should be capable of affecting world state, relationships, faction attitudes, and available stories.
- Not every choice needs to be a major branching point; subtle and delayed effects are valuable.

---

## Tracking & Consequences

Exact taxonomy, storage, and evaluation of choices will be designed later. This document is the design framework only.
