# ENVIRONMENTAL STORYTELLING

**Status:** Framework only — no specific Olomu examples invented yet.

---

## Purpose

Tell stories without (or in addition to) dialogue by using the world itself.

---

## Example Techniques (General)

- Abandoned homes
- Damaged structures
- Old roads
- Forgotten objects
- Graves
- Ruins
- Environmental changes
- NPC behavior
- Signs of conflict
- Hidden documents
- Objects with history
- Changes caused by previous events

---

## Design Notes

- Environmental storytelling should complement dialogue, not replace the need for characters and conversation where appropriate.
- Specific Olomu examples will be created only after creative decisions are approved by the project owner.
